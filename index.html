We aim to make shopping clear and convenient, with product details, secure account sign-in before ordering, and a choice of payment options at checkout. If you need help choosing an item or with an order, contact us on WhatsApp.

Orders and delivery

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Delivery arrangements

Enter a complete delivery address and a reachable phone number at checkout. We will confirm delivery availability, any delivery fee, and the expected delivery timeframe with you before dispatch. These details can vary by location and order.

Order updates

After placing an order, save the tracking reference shown in your confirmation. Use the Track Order link on this site to check its status. We may contact you using the phone number supplied to confirm delivery details.

Delivery issues

If your delivery is delayed, your address or contact details need to change, or an item arrives damaged, contact us promptly on WhatsApp with your order details.

Help with an order

Refund & Returns Policy

Please check your items when they arrive. If an item is incorrect, damaged, or you would like to request a return, contact us as soon as possible on WhatsApp and include your order reference and a description of the issue.

Return requests

Items being considered for return should be unused, unwashed, and in their original condition with labels and packaging where applicable. Contact us before sending anything back so we can confirm eligibility and return arrangements. Some product types may not be eligible for return for hygiene or other reasons; we will explain this when you contact us.

Refunds

Once an approved return is received and checked, we will let you know whether a refund or another resolution is available. Any refund method and processing timeframe will be confirmed with you. Delivery fees, if any, will be discussed as part of the resolution.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, open checkout, enter your contact and delivery details, and sign in or create an account with your email before placing the order.

How can I pay?

Checkout offers Pay on Delivery and Pay Now with MoMo or card through Paystack. Available options are shown when you place your order.

How do I track my order?

Keep the private tracking reference displayed after checkout, select Track Order, and enter the reference to see the latest status.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order reference. We will confirm whether a change or cancellation can be made based on the order's status.

How do I ask about a product or return?

Message us on WhatsApp and include the product name or order reference so we can help.
